Cookie Management

Purpose: The Cookie Management feature is designed to enhance user convenience and maintain session activity by securely storing cookies associated with users' Gmail accounts.

Functionality:

    Storing Cookies:
        When a user logs into their Gmail account through the application, the system captures and stores the authentication cookies.
        These cookies are securely stored within the system, ensuring that the user's credentials remain protected.

    Session Maintenance:
        By storing the cookies, the system can maintain the user's session activity. This means that users do not need to re-enter their email and password every time they access their Gmail account through the application.
        The stored cookies allow for seamless access to the Gmail account, providing a smoother user experience.

    Automatic Login:
        On subsequent visits, the system utilizes the stored cookies to automatically log the user into their Gmail account.
        This eliminates the need for repeated authentication, saving time and effort for the user.

    Security Considerations:
        The cookies are stored in a secure manner to prevent unauthorized access.
        The system ensures that the cookies are only used for the intended purpose of maintaining session activity and automatic login.

    User Control:
        Users have control over their stored cookies and can manage them through the application's settings.
        They can choose to delete the stored cookies if they wish to log out or switch accounts.

Benefits:

    Convenience: Users can access their Gmail accounts without repeatedly entering their credentials.
    Efficiency: Maintains session activity, allowing users to continue their work without interruptions.
    Security: Ensures that user credentials are protected while providing a seamless login experience.

Overall, the Cookie Management feature aims to provide a balance between user convenience and security by securely storing authentication cookies and maintaining session activity for Gmail accounts.
